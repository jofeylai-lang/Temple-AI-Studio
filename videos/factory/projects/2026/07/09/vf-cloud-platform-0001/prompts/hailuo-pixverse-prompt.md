# Hailuo / PixVerse Prompt

## Input Image

Use:

```text
inputs/full-body-reference.png
```

## Prompt

```text
The woman in the reference image stands naturally on a sunny beach and begins to move like a real person. She slightly shifts her posture, gently turns her head, smiles softly, and her hair and dress move in the sea breeze. Keep her identity, face, outfit, body proportions, and beach background consistent. Realistic social media video, clean lighting, natural motion.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
changed face, distorted hands, extra fingers, broken body, flickering, melting clothes, duplicate person, text, watermark, logo, cartoon, unrealistic motion
```

## Settings Suggestion

```text
Aspect ratio: 9:16
Duration: 5 seconds
Motion: medium
Style: realistic
```
