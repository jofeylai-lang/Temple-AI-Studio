# Luma Image-to-Video Prompt

## Input Image

Use:

```text
inputs/full-body-reference.png
```

## Prompt

```text
A natural beach lifestyle video of the woman in the reference photo. She stands calmly, moves subtly and realistically, her hair and dress respond to a gentle ocean breeze, and the camera slowly pushes in. Preserve her face, outfit, body shape, hairstyle, and gentle expression. Bright natural sunlight, realistic skin, clean beach background, premium personal brand video.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
face distortion, identity change, unnatural body movement, flicker, duplicated body, extra fingers, broken hands, warped dress, text, watermark, logo
```

## Settings Suggestion

```text
Duration: 5 seconds
Camera: slow push-in
Motion: subtle natural movement
Use case: brand intro / Reels / Shorts
```

## Best Use

Use Luma for natural camera motion and polished lifestyle feeling.
