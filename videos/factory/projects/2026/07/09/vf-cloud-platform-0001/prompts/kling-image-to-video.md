# Kling Image-to-Video Prompt

## Input Image

Use:

```text
inputs/full-body-reference.png
```

## Prompt

```text
A realistic full-body video of the woman in the reference image standing on a sunny beach, moving naturally like a real person. She gently shifts her weight, breathes naturally, turns her head slightly toward the camera, her hair and dress move softly in the sea breeze, and she gives a calm friendly smile. Keep her face, outfit, body proportions, hairstyle, and identity consistent with the reference image. Natural beach daylight, cinematic but realistic, subtle handheld camera movement, clean social media creator style.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
distorted face, changing identity, extra fingers, broken hands, warped body, unstable legs, duplicated person, face morphing, unnatural walking, melting clothes, flickering, low resolution, text, logo, watermark, heavy makeup, fantasy style, cartoon style
```

## Settings Suggestion

```text
Mode: Image to Video
Duration: 5 seconds
Aspect ratio: 9:16
Motion: medium
Camera: subtle push-in or natural handheld
Output: Reels / Shorts / TikTok
```

## Best Use

Use this first. It is the best candidate for natural full-body motion from one image.
