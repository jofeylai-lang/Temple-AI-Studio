# Motion Control Brief

## Desired Motion

```text
The subject stays standing in place.
She does not walk away or dance.
She subtly shifts weight from one leg to the other.
Her head turns slightly toward the camera.
Her hair and dress move gently in the ocean breeze.
She gives a soft natural smile.
The camera slowly pushes in.
```

## Avoid

```text
Large walking motion
Fast dancing
Extreme arm movement
Big body rotation
Jump cuts
Identity change
Face morphing
Hand distortion
Foot distortion
```

## Best First Test

Use subtle movement. The more extreme the motion, the more likely identity and body shape will break.
