# Runway Image-to-Video Prompt

## Input Image

Use:

```text
inputs/full-body-reference.png
```

## Prompt

```text
Create a realistic cinematic shot of the woman from the reference image standing on the beach. Preserve her identity, face, hairstyle, outfit, and natural proportions. She moves like a real person: subtle breathing, gentle head movement, soft eye contact with camera, slight body weight shift, natural fabric and hair movement from ocean wind. Keep the scene bright, clean, and realistic. Soft handheld camera movement, premium lifestyle creator video, no dramatic transformation.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
identity drift, face changes, extra limbs, deformed fingers, warped body, unnatural pose, plastic skin, flicker, morphing, duplicated person, low quality, text, watermark, logo
```

## Settings Suggestion

```text
Model: latest Gen image-to-video model available
Duration: 5 seconds
Framing: vertical 9:16 if available
Motion strength: low to medium
Camera motion: subtle push-in
```

Use Runway when you want more cinematic realism and better output polish.
