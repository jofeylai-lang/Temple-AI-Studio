# Short-Form Video Rules V1

Status: Active Production Rules

Date: 2026-07-24

Authority:

- `TEMPLE_AI_CONSTITUTION.md`

## Purpose

Convert current short-form video research into reusable Temple production rules.

This document does not copy content from Instagram, TikTok or YouTube.

It extracts production principles.

## Platform Pattern Summary

Short-form videos should be designed for:

- immediate topic recognition
- mobile-first framing
- early hook
- clear visual motion
- readable subtitles
- fast emotional confirmation
- short scene rhythm
- explicit CTA
- platform-ready vertical export

## Hook Rule

The first 1-3 seconds must answer:

```text
Why should the viewer keep watching?
```

Temple-friendly hook types:

- direct problem
- product promise
- ritual invitation
- transformation preview
- curiosity line
- sensory detail

## Scene Timing Rule

Default 24-30 second Temple product video:

| Segment | Suggested Duration |
| --- | --- |
| Hook | 1-3 seconds |
| Product reveal | 3-5 seconds |
| Feature/value sequence | 8-12 seconds |
| Spiritual/emotional value | 5-8 seconds |
| CTA/ending | 3-5 seconds |

## Subtitle Rule

Subtitles should be:

- Traditional Chinese
- short
- readable on mobile
- emotionally clear
- not overly technical
- aligned with narration

Avoid:

- dense paragraphs
- tiny text
- low contrast
- jargon
- text outside safe areas

## Camera Language Rule

Product videos should use:

- close-up detail
- slow reveal
- gentle movement
- stable framing
- product-first composition
- texture and material emphasis

Emma videos should use:

- identity-preserving face/body framing
- natural expression
- restrained camera motion
- no identity-breaking pose changes

## CTA Rule

CTA should be clear but not aggressive.

Temple-friendly CTA examples by intent:

- save for later
- message to reserve
- visit product page
- choose your ritual item
- begin your daily practice

## Retention Rule

Every scene must either:

- reveal something new
- deepen emotion
- clarify value
- move toward CTA

If a scene repeats the same information without visual or emotional progress, cut or rewrite it.

## Quality Gate Mapping

These rules map to future automatic checks:

- hook appears before second 3
- subtitle density is under threshold
- CTA exists near ending
- scene durations are within expected ranges
- video is vertical
- product appears early
- no long blank or static sections

## Definition Of Done

Short-Form Video Rules V1 is complete when future scripts, storyboards and quality checks can use it as a production standard.
